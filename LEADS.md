## LEAD-sandridge — Sandridge Road / Lake Asbury / First Coast Expressway

**Status:** unverified_lead · **Priority:** high

District 5's Lake Asbury area is undergoing rapid change: the county-funded
Sandridge Road (CR 739B) widening (~$31.6M, per the county's own project
page), a 2005 Lake Asbury Master Plan, a 2021 moratorium on new rezoning
within the master-plan area, growth pressure tied to the First Coast
Expressway, and a 2025 inter-neighborhood "connectivity" plan that was
withdrawn after being introduced. Whether specific land sales, rezonings, or
comprehensive-plan amendments in this corridor show a pattern connecting
county infrastructure spending, private landowners/developers, and BCC/
District 5 decisions is the open question.

- **Evidence available:** Official county project page for the road widening.
  Secondary news coverage of the connectivity-plan withdrawal.
- **Evidence missing:** No parcel-level ownership chain. No specific
  rezoning/comp-plan-amendment case pulled from BCC agendas. No confirmation
  of Burke's specific votes on any item in this corridor.
- **Records needed:** Property Appraiser parcel records for the corridor;
  BCC agenda/minutes for Lake Asbury Master Plan amendments and rezonings
  2019-present; the withdrawn 2025 connectivity-plan application and
  applicant; Sunbiz records for any LLC acquiring land in the corridor.

rezone 13.86 acres off CR 16A in Green Cove Springs (AG to Rural
Residential) 4-1, against staff/Planning Commission/Springs CAC
recommendations of approval. This is currently the only confirmed
Gustafson-related BCC matter found.

  Clay County land/development records, or of any relationship to Burke.
- **Records needed:** Official June 24, 2025 BCC agenda/staff report/
  minutes/video; Property Appraiser records for the CR 16A parcel and any
  other Gustafson-held parcels; Sunbiz search for Gustafson-affiliated

## LEAD-stormwater — Stormwater decision-making and Burke's role as Chair

**Status:** documented_connection · **Priority:** medium

Clay County adopted a stormwater assessment fee (impervious-surface based,
~$51-$161/yr for a single-family home) alongside hardship/veteran
exemptions. News coverage describes Board Chair Kristen Burke chairing a
contentious hearing, reported as frustrated with attendees and threatening
ejection for clapping or speaking out of turn. Dollar figures for this fee
are tracked in the main money-trail dataset (sourceId `stormwater-impact`);
this lead is about the decision-making process and Burke's role.

- **Evidence available:** Multiple secondary news accounts of hearings,
  exemption votes, and Burke's conduct as chair.
- **Evidence missing:** No official minutes/video timestamp pulled to
  independently confirm the reported conduct. No full vote history compiled.
  No confirmation of actual (vs. budgeted) stormwater spending yet.
- **Records needed:** Official BCC meeting video/minutes with timestamps for
  the assessment-adoption and exemption votes; full commissioner-by-
  commissioner vote record; actual stormwater collections/expenditures once
  available.

## LEAD-compensation — Commissioner compensation increase proposal

**Status:** unverified_lead · **Priority:** medium

Commissioner salary has been fixed at $37,000/year since 2008 per the
charter (already in the main dataset, sourceId `county-charter`). The
Charter Review Commission — a separate body from the BCC — has reportedly
discussed raising it, with secondary sources citing figures from ~$60,000
up to "triple" current pay; Charter Review Chair Courtney Connor is
reported opposed. **Whether Burke has personally advocated for or against
any specific figure has not been established — do not attribute this
proposal to her without a primary-source statement or vote.**

- **Evidence available:** Secondary news coverage describing the Charter
  Review Commission's discussion and a range of figures.
- **Evidence missing:** No primary Charter Review Commission agenda/minutes
  pulled. No Burke statement or vote found. Exact figure inconsistent
  across secondary sources.
- **Records needed:** Official Charter Review Commission agenda/minutes with
  the exact proposed figure and method; ballot-measure status; any recorded
  Burke statement or vote.
