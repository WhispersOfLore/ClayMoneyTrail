# Commissioner Compensation Timeline

Resolves LEAD-compensation using primary and secondary sources gathered
2026-09-11.

## Who introduced it

**The Clay County Charter Review Commission** — a periodic, separate
advisory body from the BCC, not Burke and not the BCC itself. No source
found attributes the original proposal to any specific individual
commissioner (BCC or CRC). **Burke's own role is limited to what's
documented below — she did not introduce this proposal.**

## Current pay structure

$37,000/year flat salary per commissioner, fixed since 2008 under the
county's Home Rule Charter (already in ClayMoneyTrail's main dataset,
`sourceId: county-charter`).

## Proposed pay structure

Tie commissioner compensation to the formula in **Florida Statute
145.031** (the state's standard formula for county-official salaries based
on population), rather than the county's own fixed charter figure. Per
secondary reporting, this would raise commissioner pay from $37,000 to
**"more than $100,000"** — described as roughly tripling current pay. (An
earlier round of coverage cited a lower ~$60,000 figure; the CRC's final,
approved ballot proposal is the FS 145.031 tie-in described here — treat
the $60,000 figure as an earlier/superseded draft, not the final proposal.)

**Ballot language (as reported):** "Shall Clay County amend its charter so
that county commissioners' compensation is limited to the salary set forth
by state law, Florida Statute 145.031?"

## Who spoke for / against it

- **Against:** Charter Review Commission Chair Courtney Connor (not a BCC
  commissioner).
- **For:** Not specifically attributed to a named individual in sources
  reviewed; general secondary-source framing describes proponents arguing
  the $37,000 figure hasn't kept pace with Florida Statute guidance on
  population-based pay, and that commissioners work "five, six, seven days
  a week" for the current salary.
