# Counter-Evidence — Evidence Against Our Own Leads

Mandatory per this pass's instructions: for every active lead, actively
search for evidence AGAINST the working theory, not just evidence for it.
Listed by lead ID from `LEADS.md`.

## LEAD-gustafson

**Working theory (implicit in the original lead):** a commissioner denied a
staff/CAC/Planning-Commission-recommended application in a way that
benefits someone or disadvantages the applicant improperly.

**Counter-evidence found:**
- Burke did **not** vote alone — the motion to deny carried **4-1**, with
  three other commissioners (Sgromolo, Compere, Condon) also voting to
  deny. This was not a lone or narrowly-partisan action.
- **Jim Renninger**, not Burke, was the only commissioner who voted to
  approve — meaning the Board was not unanimous in either direction, and
  Burke's position was the majority position, not an outlier.
- The applicant is a **13-year homesteaded resident** subdividing her own
  land, not an outside developer who recently acquired the property ahead
  of a favorable vote — the property chain shows no suspicious timing (see
  `SANDRIDGE_PROPERTY_CHAIN.md`).
- Chairman Condon **offered the applicant a path to reapply** (smaller lot
  size or PUD) rather than a flat rejection — consistent with a
  discretionary land-use judgment call, not hostility to the applicant.
- No campaign contribution, business relationship, or documented connection
  between any Gustafson and Burke has been found in either direction
  (`PATTERN_MATRIX.md`).
- Burke's own public campaign messaging (Clay Today, 2023) frames her
  re-election around "continu[ing] to fight against overdevelopment" —
  denying a 13-lot subdivision request is consistent with that stated,
  public policy position, not an ad hoc or targeted decision.

**Assessment:** the evidence gathered so far is consistent with a
commissioner exercising ordinary land-use discretion in line with a
publicly stated growth-management position, joined by a majority of the
Board. Nothing found supports a benefit-to-Burke theory for this specific
vote.

## LEAD-campaign-contributors

**Working theory:** developers/vendors/contractors donate to Burke and
later receive favorable treatment.

**Counter-evidence found:**
- Of ~120 itemized contributions across both cycles, only a small handful
  carry a development-adjacent occupation (The Vector Companies Inc —
  "Affordable housing"; two attorneys). **None has been matched to any BCC
  matter, contract, or land-use application reviewed in this
  investigation** — the theory has had two full cycles of data to find
  support in and hasn't.
- The one applicant/agent pair actually documented before the BCC this
  pass (Sheryl Gustafson, Danielle Kitchens) **does not appear anywhere**
  in either campaign-finance dataset.
- The single largest recurring vendor relationship (Kronmiller Consulting)
  is a **campaign-administration vendor** (treasurer/accounting), not a
  county contractor — a category the theory doesn't implicate.

**Assessment:** no support found yet for a donor→favorable-treatment
pattern; the campaign-finance dataset and the one documented land-use vote
this pass simply don't overlap.

## LEAD-burke-chiropractic

**Working theory:** an undisclosed family business relationship exists that
could create a conflict of interest.

**Counter-evidence found:**
- The Sunbiz record for the entity matching the name "Oakleaf Family
  Chiropractic" (the business Burke's own bio says was opened for her
  brother) lists **no Burke as an officer** — if this is meant to show
  Burke has an ownership stake, the primary corporate record doesn't
  support that (see `PATTERN_MATRIX.md` — filed as CONFLICTING EVIDENCE,
  not confirmed either way).
- **No BCC matter, permit, or county action involving either chiropractic
  business has been found** — even if a family relationship exists, there
  is currently no county decision for it to conflict with.
- A same-named "Burke Chiropractic LLC" was found and affirmatively
  **disproven** as connected (different city, different family) —
  evidence that a surname match alone does not establish a relationship,
  which cuts against assuming the "brother" claim implies any particular
  business without direct confirmation.

**Assessment:** even taking Burke's own stated family connection to Oakleaf
Family Chiropractic at face value, no county decision has been found that
it would conflict with — the underlying "conflict of interest" premise has
no documented county action to attach to yet.

## LEAD-stormwater

**Working theory:** Burke pushed through an unpopular fee over strong
public opposition.

**Counter-evidence found:**
- **The actual ordinance-adoption vote (July 28, 2026) was unanimous, 5-0**,
  with only **one public speaker**, who asked a cost question rather than
  opposing the measure outright.
- **Burke did not make either motion** — Vice-Chairman John Sgromolo moved
  both the ordinance and the resolution; Burke's role was limited to
  chairing.
- The earlier, genuinely contentious hearings described in secondary press
  coverage appear to be **separate, earlier meetings** (exemption debates),
  not the meeting that actually enacted the assessment — see
  `STORMWATER_MASTER_TIMELINE.md` for this distinction.

**Assessment:** the primary record of the actual adoption vote does not
support a "Burke forced through an unpopular measure" narrative — that
specific vote was calm and unanimous. Whether an earlier hearing was
genuinely contentious, and what if anything changed between then and the
final vote, remains an open, separate question.

## LEAD-compensation

**Working theory:** Burke is personally driving a large pay increase for
herself.

**Counter-evidence found:**
- The proposal originates from the **Charter Review Commission**, a
  separate body Burke does not sit on by default (BCC members do not
  automatically serve on the CRC).
- **No source found attributes the proposal, its introduction, or advocacy
  for it to Burke specifically** — the named participants in secondary
  coverage are CRC Chair Courtney Connor (opposed) and unnamed "proponents."
- The BCC's only described role is to forward CRC-approved ballot language
  **unchanged** to the Supervisor of Elections — a ministerial function,
  not a discretionary vote Burke could personally drive.

**Assessment:** no evidence found connects Burke to this proposal in any
active way; the working theory as stated has no support and should not be
repeated without a primary source naming her involvement.

## LEAD-sandridge

**Working theory:** county infrastructure spending in Burke's home area
benefits nearby landowners/developers, including possibly Burke herself.

**Counter-evidence found:**
- The one fully-documented land-use matter in this general area (Gustafson,
  technically a different sub-area — CR 16-A/The Springs, not the
  Sandridge corridor itself) was **denied**, not approved — the opposite
  of a "waved through for a connected party" pattern.
- Burke's own residence (Russell Landing) is in the general Lake Asbury
  area, which explains geographic donor clustering (Sandridge Road donors)
  without requiring a developer-benefit theory — an incumbent's own
  neighbors donating to her campaign is a mundane explanation that fits the
  same facts.
- No parcel on the Sandridge Road corridor itself has yet been traced to
  any BCC vote, campaign donor, or Burke-connected entity — the theory has
  no documented instance to point to yet, for or against.

**Assessment:** insufficient data either way; the counter-evidence found so
far (a denial, not an approval, in the adjacent area; a mundane explanation
for the donor cluster) weakens rather than supports the working theory, but
the Sandridge corridor itself remains largely unexamined.
