## Developers, builders, engineering firms, attorneys, contractors — cross-reference status

Of the entities searched, only two carry an occupation/description
suggesting development-adjacent work: **The Vector Companies Inc**
("Affordable housing," 2024 only) and the two law firms/attorneys (William
& Yoho PA, Barry Ansbacher — legal generally, not confirmed as land-use
counsel specifically). **None of the three has been matched to any BCC
land-use, contract, or procurement matter reviewed in this investigation.**
This is a genuine negative result, not a gap — recorded per the mission's
instruction that the system must also identify evidence that weakens a
hypothesis (here, the hypothesis that development-adjacent money flows
directly into Burke's campaign finds no support in what has been reviewed
so far).
