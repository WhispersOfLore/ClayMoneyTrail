# Burke Decision Timeline, 2020–2026

**Status: seeded, not exhaustive.** Clay County's BCC held roughly 25–30
regular meetings per year across Burke's 2020–2026 tenure (~150+ meetings
total). Reading every meeting's full minutes was outside this pass's
budget. This file contains every significant District 5 / Burke land-use,
infrastructure, or policy vote **actually confirmed against primary-source
minutes** so far, plus an honest map of what years/meetings remain
unreviewed. Treat gaps as `records_required`, not as "nothing happened."

Every entry below traces to an official BCC minutes document (PrimeGov),
cited with its `meetingTemplateId` and video timestamp.

## Confirmed events

### 2024-10-22 — Three large District 5 industrial-rezoning applications continued

**Status: verified_fact (procedural vote only — no merits vote occurred)**

Agenda items 27, 28, 29: three linked FLUM/rezoning applications, all
"(District 5, Comm. Burke)," all handled together:

| Item | Acreage | Change requested |
|---|---|---|
| COMP 24-0016 / ZON 24-0019 | 1.72 acres | Agricultural (AG) → Industrial (IND) |
| COMP 24-0017 / ZON 24-0023 | 41.75 acres | Agricultural Residential (AR) → Heavy Industrial (IB) |
| COMP 24-0018 / ZON 24-0024 | 32.79 acres | Agricultural Residential (AR) → Heavy Industrial (IB) |

**~76.26 combined acres** proposed for industrial rezoning in District 5.
Agent: Kelly Hartwig (3420 Wall Road, Green Cove Springs) — applicant's own
name not stated in the minutes text pulled. Public hearing opened by
Chairman James Renninger at 6:05pm; two speakers (Patricia Ross, thanking
the applicant for addressing neighbor concerns; Van Royal, reserving
comment). **The applicant requested, and the Board unanimously granted
(5-0, Compere/Condon), a continuance of all three items to January 28,
2025** — no vote on the merits occurred at this meeting.
Source: official BCC minutes, PrimeGov meetingTemplateId 392 (meeting id
81), video 2:01:58–2:07:46.

### 2025-06-24 — Gustafson comprehensive-plan amendment denied

**Status: verified_fact.** Full detail already documented in
`SANDRIDGE_PROPERTY_CHAIN.md` and `data/investigations/evidence.json`
(EV-010/EV-011). Summary: Vice-Chairman Kristen Burke personally moved to
deny Sheryl Gustafson's COMP 25-0010 (13.86 acres, 2005 S CR 16-A) against
staff/CAC/Planning-Commission recommendations of approval; seconded by

### 2026-07-28 — Stormwater Utility and Assessment Ordinance adopted

**Status: verified_fact.** Full detail in `STORMWATER_MASTER_TIMELINE.md`.
